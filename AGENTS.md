# Lumen: guide for AI assistants

The full guide is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): read it first and follow it (it is written for any assistant, not only
Claude Code). Most people ask in plain words, like "Install Lumen on my TV at 192.168.1.50. I live in Lisbon.",
and don't know what a terminal is: explain each step simply, and ask before anything that changes their TV or computer.

Moving the owner's own TV from Free My TV to Lumen: [INSTALL-MY-TV.md](INSTALL-MY-TV.md) is their step-by-step, written
for an assistant that does as much as it can itself. When they point you to it, follow it.
